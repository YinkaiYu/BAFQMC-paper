# Story, Clarity, And Evidence

Give each paragraph a clear physical purpose. Establish the motivation, explain the
obstacle or question, and lead readers through the idea, reasoning, result, and
consequences in the order the argument needs. Preserve the bridge between sentences
and paragraphs.

Readers have no access to our collaboration history. Remove prose that answers a
private request, narrates editing, displays terminology for its own sake, or
preemptively defends against hypothetical objections.

## Reader Burden

Use familiar theoretical-physics language. Connect mathematical derivations with
their physical meaning in whichever order makes the argument clearest. Show the
steps needed for understanding and explain unfamiliar concepts as they arise.
Avoid obscure mathematical jargon, invented labels, internal shorthand, and lists
of specialist terms.

Introduce variables, symbols, and definitions only when necessary. Explain their
meaning locally and avoid sending readers backward for a short-lived definition.
Concision should remove repetition and detours while preserving the physical
picture and logical steps.

## Confidence And Correctness

State established contributions directly and confidently. Avoid unnecessary
defensive weakening that obscures their importance. Preserve assumptions and
limitations where they actually affect the result or its interpretation.

During research, complete the necessary derivations and checks. Decide what to
show in the Letter only after the reasoning is sound. The SM should teach the
derivation and implementation in enough detail for readers to follow and reproduce
the work.

Prefer positive sentences. Avoid "not X, but Y", similar rhetorical reversals,
semicolon chains, and excessive colon constructions.

## Evidence

Match evidence to the claim: references support prior results, derivations support
mathematical conclusions, benchmarks test implementations, and schematics explain
concepts. Do not substitute one for another.

Research broadly enough to understand the question, then write selectively.
Citations should support the physical story without forcing a literature catalogue
into the paper. Verify bibliography metadata and maintain the required local PDF
cache.

Use the current manuscript as the editing context. Historical drafts and style
examples are useful references, not immutable rules.
