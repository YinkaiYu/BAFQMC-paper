# Reference Verification

Use this before adding or modifying any BibTeX entry.

## Required Metadata

Verify each field against arXiv, APS, DOI/Crossref, or publisher pages:

- author list
- title
- journal
- volume
- page or article number
- year
- DOI
- arXiv identifier, if cited or relevant

Do not infer metadata from memory.

## Citation Strategy

- The final bibliography may be broad, but every citation must serve a claim.
- Use citation clusters for established background.
- Add references when a compressed statement relies on known results or a reader may want a route into the literature.
- Distinguish world-line/SSE bosonic sign-free context, fermionic AFQMC sign-free criteria, bosonic AFQMC predecessors, reflection positivity, TRS/Kramers arguments, and benchmark/numerical-method references.

## Reporting To Author

After adding or changing references, report:

- citation keys added or edited
- source used for verification
- any uncertainty or metadata mismatch

The author must be prompted to review new references because bibliography errors are unacceptable.
