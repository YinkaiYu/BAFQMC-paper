# Review Questions

Use these questions when reviewing the relevant text; they are not a mandatory
separate audit for every edit.

- Does the physical motivation lead naturally to the technical argument and result?
- Can a physicist outside the immediate subfield follow the explanation?
- Are unfamiliar concepts explained before use, with economical notation and no
  unexplained logical jumps?
- Does the prose confidently convey the contribution without unnecessary defensive
  qualifications?
- Does every sentence serve the reader rather than respond to private feedback?
- Are the derivations correct and sufficiently detailed in the SM?
- Do claims, assumptions, equations, captions, benchmarks, and citations agree?
- Are bibliography metadata verified and all cited external papers cached locally?
- Do figure references match the actual source and rendered figures?
- Does the changed passage fit its surrounding text?

Report concrete findings relevant to the task. Avoid rewriting unrelated mature
text or imposing a fixed narrative template.
